Connections worth examining

Ezekiel 47:1–12 describes life-giving waters and trees whose leaves are for healing. This provides an important prophetic parallel to Revelation 22, but Ezekiel does not call them the tree of life.

Research note: Revelation 22:14 has a known manuscript variant (“wash their robes” versus “do his commandments”). Revelation 22:19 also has a “tree of life” / “book of life” textual variant and is reserved for further research. The diagram avoids resolving these variants by visual design alone.

Method: Direct mentions are distinguished from figurative wisdom uses and from broader theological parallels. Summaries are paraphrases; read the full passages in context. Exported graphics show the reference map, not the full notes.
