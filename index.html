Scripture, context & interpretive limits

Ezekiel 47:1–12 describes life-giving water and trees with healing leaves, a significant literary parallel to Revelation 22, but does not name the tree of life. Revelation 22:14 contains a textual variant concerning those who have access to the tree. Revelation 22:19 has a “tree of life” / “book of life” textual variant and is not mapped as an undisputed direct reference. Every illustration is artistic rather than a biblical description of appearance.
